What is Backlog Hero?

Backlog Hero is a place to virtually manage your game collection. Keep your backlog updated, rate the games you've played, track your playtime, and add upcoming titles to your wishlist. Share your gaming journey with friends by following each other and staying up-to-date on the latest play sessions.

Track your personal game collection

Log any and every game you've played, are currently playing, or want to play. Be as detailed as you want — mark your progress, rate titles out of five stars, and organize everything with custom statuses like Playing, Backlog, and Completed.
